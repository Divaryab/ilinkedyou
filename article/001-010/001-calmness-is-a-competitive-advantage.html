Why clear thinking becomes harder in noisy environments — and how calmness changes the quality of decisions.

Modern life rewards speed, urgency, and constant stimulation. But clear thinking often emerges from the opposite conditions.

Calmness is not passivity. It is the ability to remain mentally organized while everything around you becomes noisy.
