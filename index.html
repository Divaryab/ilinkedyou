Before we start

A few common questions

Clarity from the beginning

Who is this service for?

International buyers considering new-build or under-construction property in Tbilisi, especially those who are buying remotely or want structured support throughout the process.

What does the service cost the buyer?

For projects we cooperate with, the developer pays our commission. Independent services such as a lawyer or technical inspector, when needed, are priced separately.

Do you guarantee profit or the developer’s handover date?

No. We do not guarantee investment returns, price growth, or a developer’s delivery date. Our role is to provide clear information, structured follow-up, and buyer support through the process.

How do you follow up after the purchase?

Information, documents, photos, and updates related to your property are organized in your private client portal.

How do I start?

With a short conversation about your goal, budget, and purchase timeline. From there, we define the most useful next step.
