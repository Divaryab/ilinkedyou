About

Hi, I’m Ali.

Like many people, I spent years searching for someone who would truly see me, listen without judgment, and stay beside me while I moved toward the goals that mattered to me; even if those goals later sounded silly to others, or even to myself.

The goal itself is rarely the most important thing. What matters more is feeling understood by someone who genuinely cares, pays attention, and wants to see you move forward.

At iLinkedYou, the goal is not perfection. It’s staying connected to what matters to you, even through the difficult and uncertain parts of the journey.

I’ll simply be there beside you, step by step. Without pressure. Without trying to control who you become.
