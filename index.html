Before you buy

Answers to the questions that matter

Clear answers before you choose a project

Why not buy directly from the developer?

You can. A developer naturally presents its own project. I start with your needs and budget, compare a few suitable options, and continue supporting you after you choose.

How do you choose the right project for me?

We clarify your budget, buying goal, timeline, and preferred payment plan. I then compare the most relevant options side by side, so you can choose between a few suitable projects rather than work through a long list.

What does your service cost me?

For projects where I work with the developer, the developer pays my commission. Independent services such as a lawyer or technical inspector, when needed, are paid separately.

How can I manage the purchase if I live outside Georgia?

Much of the process can be handled remotely. Your case status, documents, reports, and updates are organized in your private client portal, and I let you know in advance about any step that requires your presence or a separate action.

Do you stay involved after the contract is signed?

Yes. My work does not end at signing. I continue tracking project updates, documents, and next steps through preparation for handover.

Do you provide legal, technical, or project guarantees?

No. I am not your lawyer, technical inspector, or guarantor of the project. When needed, I can connect you with an independent specialist. I also do not guarantee returns, price growth, or the developer’s handover date.

What if I’m not sure I’m ready to buy yet?

Start with a short conversation. You do not need to have made a final decision; first we clarify your situation, then decide whether there is a useful next step.
